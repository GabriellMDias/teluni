## Pilot and public trial

The first candidate pilot may receive **30 free software days for up to two screens**, starting only after functional setup and the operator's explicit start confirmation. Hardware, travel, installation beyond the included onboarding and content-production services are separate. Confirm participation/physical devices under PH-08, written/comparably attributable commercial agreement under PH-18 and privacy/readiness before real use. Owner approval is not customer acceptance. Extensions must be bounded, justified and recorded.

Present intended post-pilot price/conditions before starting. Conversion requires explicit commercial acceptance and the first confirmed payment; no unexpected automatic charging, silent lifetime discount or indefinite free continuation. Collect core usability, offline/recovery and device results alongside physical storage, transfer/processing, onboarding/support time, customer objections and willingness to continue. Public use of the client's name, logo, images or testimonial needs separate permission.

Public eligible verified companies receive an automatically activated **14-day trial**, starting at company activation, with a visible end instant and notices. No card is required and there is no permanent free subscription. The trial includes up to **two screens, one branch, five members and 2 GiB of original media**, plus **10 GiB of player media transfers** during the trial. Scope/abuse checks and qualified global capacity are admission prerequisites; do not substitute routine manual company approval for the PH-11 signup decision. Publish only an offer the host can actually admit safely.

At trial end, disallow new publishing, uploads and device enrollment. An authenticated, otherwise-authorized account may retain explicitly permitted contracting, account recovery and export paths in a **commercially restricted** state. This is distinct from a security-suspended account/company: commercial exceptions cannot bypass identity or security suspension. One evaluation per legitimate organization, with attributable assisted exceptions and proportionate abuse controls; email-domain/IP/name similarity alone neither proves shared ownership nor merges companies. No automatic trial extension; a justified exception must be bounded/audited.

## Screen licenses and pricing

One screen license authorizes one player/device for one independently programmed playback output. Multiple regions, images/videos, catalog views or simultaneous functions within that output do not incur separate screen fees. The application does not claim to detect the number of physical panels attached through HDMI cloning. Multiple independent outputs need their own supported profile and explicit licensing before sale.

Customers contract a fixed license capacity per billing period. Assignment occupies a license until explicitly deallocated; connectivity/heartbeat does not create, cancel or meter the subscription. Turning off a TV does not reduce the contracted package. Separate companies do not silently share a minimum fee or discounts.

For the initial validation offer, one or two screens cost the BRL 79.90 company minimum. For `n > 2`, the proposed monthly total is BRL 79.90 + BRL 29.90 × (`n` − 2). Example totals: three screens BRL 109.80, four BRL 139.70 and ten BRL 319.10. Automatic table-based offers stop at ten licenses initially; larger fleets need a capacity/cost-qualified quote, without an automatic volume discount. Ten is an offer boundary, not a platform architecture limit; the reference 100-player benchmark is not measured host capacity.

Replace a failed player through an explicit license transfer and revocation, without an extra replacement charge. An unreachable old player may retain its already-issued authorization until expiry, at most seven days. Allow one pending replacement per license, report any overlap and prevent repeated replacement rotation; this is an explicit recovery exception, not an unlimited extra license. Cross-company reassignment still needs verified wipe/reset under the release security policy.

The single initial plan includes the approved capabilities that have actually been delivered. Never sell roadmap features as available. Do not gate HTTPS, isolation, required MFA, recovery, security fixes or reliable offline operation as paid upgrades. Do not separately charge per member/branch or per simultaneous screen function initially; explicit qualified technical limits still apply to members, branches, records, fields, layouts and imports. "No per-user fee" does not mean infinite resources. Trial-specific limits remain distinct.

These reference allowances do not decide codec/resolution/rendition processing profiles, source-file acceptance, CPU quotas, global concurrency, aggregate tenant admission, disk reserve or control/telemetry budgets. Input minutes are not CPU minutes. Actual bounds and configuration/contracts remain D-05/D-12, PH-10/PH-18 and PJ-27. Public admission and quota-dependent operations must remain unavailable until safe finite limits and resource reservations are defined and verified; unknown capacity is not an unlimited default.

Count each original once per company rather than once per playlist; an archived original remains occupied storage until safe physical purge. Account for actual transmitted media bytes, including retransmissions. Internal failure retries must not silently double-charge processing. Protect sufficient physical capacity for renditions, history, current/recovery/staging generations, deduplicated references and backups; do not present the customer's original-media quota as total host disk demand. Original bytes in protected history still require clear accounting/reference handling before any quota is published.

Warn at **80% and 100%** of qualified commercial usage. Reject new work exceeding the applicable quota or offer an explicitly accepted expansion; no automatic overage bill, implicit tolerance, protected-resource deletion or arbitrary interruption of issued programming. A purchase never overrides a safe technical limit. Plan/reserve an authorized generation's transfers before starting; do not sever an already-authorized download halfway solely because a counter crossed its threshold. If a new generation cannot be admitted, preserve the last valid permitted playback and show pending/failure status. Platform-caused failure needs explicit remediation rather than an undisclosed extra charge.

License/plan increases require explicit confirmation and a demonstrated prorated charge for the remaining cycle; reductions take effect at renewal, with explicit choice of licenses to deactivate. Specify exact monetary rounding/calendar rules in the reviewed future contract. Warn about usage over the intended lower quota and resolve it before confirming reduction; never erase protected data or choose arbitrary TVs to make a downgrade fit.

## Hardware, onboarding and support

Customers initially supply compatible displays/players, power and network. Teluni must qualify the devices it advertises under PH-08 and diagnose its software faults; do not promise all Smart TVs are supported. Hardware resale/lease is not selected.

Include one remote onboarding/training session up to **60 minutes**. Quote onsite work, travel, physical installation, network/electrical services and extraordinary setup separately before acceptance; any first-pilot concession must be explicit. Include delivered tools/templates and usage guidance, not recurring artwork, catalog entry, campaign production or outsourced daily content operation. Paid AI and unlimited human work are not included by implication. Record the cost of included onboarding too.

P23/P24 were conditional recommendations. **Email/form are the baseline record channels; WhatsApp is optional only after actual coverage is confirmed.** No phone number/address/account is invented. Actual operator availability, support window and calendar remain **unknown under PH-18**, rather than asserting a 09:00–18:00 or 24/7 commitment. Use explicitly arranged pilot support while coverage is being established; before any public support offer, publish only the verified window and confront it with restaurant evening/weekend needs.

The selected target is a **useful first response within one business day of the established coverage calendar**, not an automated receipt or fixed resolution deadline. It cannot be advertised as an unconditional service commitment before that coverage is verified. Prioritize critical incidents and communicate according to real coverage; no formal availability/resolution SLA is sold at this stage. Repeated support demand calls for product improvements, training or offer review, not an arbitrary bug-support cutoff. Content access continues to require PH-11 scoped consent, expiry and the support person's real identity.

Explain residential power/network limitations, maintenance and incident communication before the pilot. Normal playback can continue from a valid local generation, but login, publishing, recovery and current diagnostics still depend on services/network. Formal SLA offers require measured compatible infrastructure and support; internal targets are not a commercial guarantee.

## Payment, expiry, cancellation and retention

Start with **monthly prepayment**, explicit billing dates/license quantity and **Pix/bank transfer with attributable manual reconciliation**. A screenshot alone does not prove settled payment. Only an explicitly authorized platform billing operator may confirm settlement or change commercial grants; company administrators may request/manage their own contracted capacity/cancellation without marking themselves paid. Concrete capabilities remain D-12 review, not implemented role identifiers. No bank destination, payment-provider integration, tax treatment or fiscal issuer is selected here. PH-19 must establish the appropriate billing/fiscal process before charging. Gateway/card automation remains future; basic commercial authorization cannot be deferred just because invoicing is manual.

Late-payment policy: notices and **seven days of commercial grace after the due date**. Thereafter deny new publications/enrollment and new commercial playback renewals, while allowing only explicitly authorized regularization/recovery/export paths for otherwise valid accounts. Maintain the security-suspension distinction. Do not invent penalties/interest or silent billable continuation; final conditions require review before contracting.

For trial, paid-period expiry/cancellation and applicable grace, future player authorization must be bounded by **both** the known commercial playback deadline and the maximum seven-day offline renewal interval. The shorter bound wins. Do not retroactively claim that an unreachable device received a newly shortened deadline, instantly stopped or erased its cache. Existing valid leases may survive an unreachable revocation until their previously issued deadline. On reconnect, reconcile current payment/authorization and content validity; no reset of an expired commercial period or bypass through old snapshots. These are required behaviors to qualify, not an implemented credential format.

Offer simple monthly cancellation without a minimum-stay penalty for future renewals; normally retain the already-paid period to its end, subject to legitimate security controls. Show the effective date/license impact and export path. Refunds, exceptions and applicable rights belong to transparent reviewed terms, not a fabricated legal conclusion or waiver. No annual prepayment/discount is offered initially. Do not silently stack pilot, volume or referral discounts.

Select a **30-day product window for authorized reactivation/export** after commercial end, without edit/publication. This is not a promise to erase every byte on day 31. Reconcile PH-13 personal-data/audit/backup obligations, the at-least-90-day publication-history target and protected active/staging/recovery references. Distinguish limited account access, physical deletion and offline cache erasure. Measure the cost of closed-trial retention and stop new admission when safe capacity is unavailable.

## Economics, market and readiness

Initial market is **Brazil/BRL**, with internationalization retained and no silent currency conversion. Foreign sales, annual plans, automated billing, recurring commissions and financial referral rewards require later evaluation. Record partner interest without a current incentive; referrals grant no access to customer data or shared human credentials.

No new recurring procurement is authorized now. With received revenue or a demonstrated operating need, prepare a total-cost budget and a separately approved procurement/migration proposal. Residential hosting, backup, identity and reliable email requirements are not waived by zero procurement budget; unresolved readiness prevents operation/admission. Revenue alone neither proves capacity nor authorizes spending.

Use an **at least 70% steady-state contribution-margin target** as an owner-approved validation goal. For this exercise, deduct applicable taxes/fees and attributable variable costs including valued recurring support; keep onboarding, fixed costs and investment recovery separately visible and do not double-count them. Value the owner's time initially at **BRL 50/hour as a stated analytical assumption**, not measured salary or an external-service price. Fixed costs belong in break-even analysis; cash increment, economic allocation and sunk investment are distinct.

At BRL 79.90 and a 70% target, the deductible-cost budget would be BRL 23.97/month/company. This is arithmetic, not a measured cost or proven profit. The pilot must determine whether actual taxes, transfer/processing, support and other attributable costs fit; revise price/offer before expanding if they do not. Unknown taxes or own labor are never zero by default. Qualified fiscal/legal review precedes charging/public terms; source Apache-2.0 licensing does not replace SaaS terms or fiscal documentation.
