## Scope and acceptance rules

**Decided:** complete the company-to-screen journey with tenant-safe remote management, images/videos, configurable data catalogs, freeform simultaneous content, regional schedules, offline Android TV playback and monitoring. The owner prioritizes intended product capability/quality over reducing scope solely to fit an MVP label. The historical `mvp.md` path remains the stable requirements entry point.

Every capability below is a requirement, not implemented functionality. The [plan](implementation-plan.md) owns delivery status; [release policies](release-policies.md) own business behavior and the questionnaire decision record; [acceptance targets](release-targets.md) own numeric limits and qualification goals. PH-09 policy/target selection is complete as of 2026-10-18. No hardware, pilot or performance qualification is inferred.

Price publication/expiry and unavailable-item defaults work from consistent offline snapshots; customization cannot execute arbitrary code or cross tenant boundaries. |

No billing from heartbeat, partial download cutoff, unlimited defaults, surprise charge or security-suspension bypass. PH-18/PH-19 qualify the offer; no gateway or automatic invoicing is required. |

Managed campaigns remain future scope; scheduled content and published price-validity rules provide the initial promotion behavior without approving a separate campaign engine.

## Release acceptance journey

1. Establish two synthetic companies through verified enrollment with responsible administrators, differently scoped branches/operators and qualified Android TV devices. Exercise IA-01–IA-16 login/linking/recovery/session/revocation/approval/support cases and API/media/catalog allow/deny boundaries; no implicit platform content privilege.
2. Pair devices, create a cross-branch group and an explicit individual exception. Upload media; configure a custom weekly-agenda catalog and product/price configuration. Compose independent and coordinated areas, regional schedules and an optional default; preview and publish A.
3. Verify local multi-video/audio/catalog rendering under the qualified profile. Edit drafts without changing A. Save a price and verify automatic consistent publication from published dependencies without leaking unrelated drafts; test concurrent edits, duplicate delivery and failed/unknown outcomes.
4. Publish B while playback continues. Cut network/power at download/staging/activation boundaries and verify complete valid A or B. Isolate a runtime regional fault without confusing it with incomplete-generation activation. Check protected references and insufficient disk outcomes.
5. Exercise schedule/date/DST/promotion/no-match/pagination and regional-page timing offline. Test the trusted-clock and authorization deadlines, restart/recovery and the expected safe neutral states.
6. Reconnect and reconcile observed state. Unlink/revoke while offline, then verify cleanup before cross-company pairing. Exercise historical recovery without expired offers or resurrected credentials and validate controlled protocol/app updates.
7. Exercise CP-01–CP-12 commercial trial/license/quota, reconciled-payment and grace/cancellation cases, preserving independent identity permission and valid offline deadlines. Rehearse database-plus-media restore and endpoint migration in isolation. Record target-linked load/usability/hardware evidence, customer acceptance, real costs/support coverage and residual risk before real-customer acceptance; billing readiness remains PH-19.

Evidence includes automated contract/security/integration/browser tests, representative hardware/power/network tests, restore results and user observation. Delivery can be incremental; an operational release requires the complete applicable acceptance set. Passing foundation validation only checks currently implemented foundation/docs.

## Explicitly outside the MVP

The historical heading remains a stable route; initial scope now includes freeform composition, configurable catalogs and their approved commercial defaults. Further unreviewed widgets, managed campaigns, external ERP/POS/partner integration APIs/webhooks/imports, automated billing/payment-gateway subscriptions, white-label, AI, playback analytics, general remote-control tools, impersonation, synchronization across separate screens and alternative players remain future unless independently approved. PH-11 separately selects scoped consent-based human support, custom access profiles and optional independent publication approvals for the initial release; custom profiles exist for the current organization capabilities, and the other policies remain unimplemented. Generic fields do not approve arbitrary executable plugins, unrestricted data collection or all roadmap capabilities.
