[Requirements](mvp.md) · [Release policies and decision authority](release-policies.md#ph-09-decision-record) · [Implementation plan](implementation-plan.md)

## Status and measurement rules

**Decided for qualification on 2026-10-18:** these values consolidate owner answers and explicitly delegated choices. They are targets/limits for the future product, not measurements, commercial entitlements, SLAs or evidence that any Android TV/residential host supports them. All product qualification evidence below is **pending**; passing the Orion foundation validation does not satisfy these targets.

Ingestion limits are configurable engineering defaults, not plan quotas. Device profiles may exceed the reference target only after testing; do not impose its numbers as a universal editor/architecture ceiling. Record hardware/model, OS/player/backend/protocol versions, renderer/media profile, storage, test data, browser/network/host, timestamps and failure conditions for each result. PH-08 and PH-10 own missing physical/environment facts.

## Retention targets

| ID    | Selected operational retention                                                                                                           | Requirement / source                                                                     |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AT-17 | Recoverable archive/trash for at least 30 days before purging media or archived catalog records/definitions with no protected references | FR-04/FR-11/NF-02; delegated P26 and lifecycle defaults for the custom-catalog expansion |
| AT-18 | Inactive publication history recoverable for at least 90 days                                                                            | FR-07/NF-02; delegated P26                                                               |
| AT-19 | Player retains active and previous valid generations and resources of a valid staging attempt                                            | FR-07/FR-08/NF-02; owner P19 and delegated P26                                           |
| AT-20 | Detailed operational reports: 30-day default, minimized/redacted and subject to the separate privacy/retention review                    | FR-09/NF-05; delegated P26; PH-13                                                        |

These periods never permit deleting an active, staging, recovery or otherwise protected referenced resource. Reconciliation, the offline authorization envelope and safe garbage collection remain mandatory. A restored publication still obeys content expiry and authorization. These are operational selections, not a legal retention policy for personal data or security audit; PH-13 remains pending.

## Required evidence

Implement tests at the owning boundary and link results back to these IDs and FR/NF requirements: schema/value/reference validation; cross-tenant/branch allow/deny cases; publication/price retry and ordering; shared preview/player fixtures; offline/power/clock and region-fault scenarios; qualified media/rendering profiles; load traces; and observed user tasks. Reference protection and expiry must be verified during restoration, not just normal playback.

PJ-03/PJ-08–PJ-14/PJ-20/PJ-21 deliver this evidence incrementally. PH-09 completes the owner's policy/target selection; it does not complete those implementation/qualification tasks or the separate hardware/operations/identity/privacy human actions. Any target the selected hardware cannot meet must be reported and explicitly revised or satisfied using a qualified profile, never silently weakened.
